Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity. A typical home system in Lahore has 10 panels and covers most of the daytime load. The panels last 25 years with little maintenance. Net metering lets owners sell extra power back to the grid. The upfront cost is high, but the payback period is usually four to six years. Batteries add cost but keep the lights on during load shedding. Cleaning the panels twice a month keeps output high in dusty weather. Most families start with a 5 kilowatt system and add panels later if they need more power.
